  <what-built>
CSS + JS changes implementing:

- header::before bleed fix for viewports wider than 1200px
- header.scrolled box-shadow that appears only when scrolled
- Passive scroll listener toggling .scrolled class
- Tab switch handler resetting scroll to top and removing .scrolled before renderAll()
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Open the app in Chrome. Switch to Chrome DevTools device emulation, select a mobile preset (e.g. iPhone 12 Pro, 390px width).

**Check 1 — HEADER-01: Header sticks on all 8 tabs**
For each of the 8 tabs (Dashboard, Expenses, Income, Debts, Payoff, Assets, Childcare, Settings):
1. Switch to the tab
2. Scroll down past several items
3. Confirm the header (with the app title and toolbar) remains fixed at the top — it does NOT scroll away with the content

Expected: Header is always visible at top of viewport regardless of scroll position.

**Check 2 — HEADER-02: Scroll shadow appears only when scrolled**
On any content-heavy tab (Expenses or Income):
1. Confirm no shadow is visible on the header when the page is at the very top (window.scrollY === 0)
2. Scroll down slightly (any amount > 0)
3. Confirm a shadow appears on the bottom edge of the header
4. Scroll back to the very top
5. Confirm the shadow disappears again
6. Repeat briefly in dark mode (toggle theme if available) — shadow should also be visible in dark mode

Expected: Shadow is absent at top, present while scrolled, gone again after returning to top.

**Check 3 — HEADER-03 (UX): Tab switch resets scroll to top**
1. On Expenses or Income tab, scroll down to mid-page
2. Switch to any other tab
3. Confirm the new tab opens at the top of the page — no mid-scroll landing
4. Confirm no scroll shadow flicker (shadow should be absent immediately on the freshly switched tab)

Expected: Every tab switch instantly shows the top of the content with no shadow.

**Check 4 — MONNAV-01: Month navigator sticks below header with no overlap**
On the Income or Expenses tab:
1. Scroll down until the month navigator (◀ March 2026 ▶) reaches the header
2. Confirm the month navigator sticks immediately below the header with no gap and no overlap
3. Confirm month-nav content is not partially hidden behind the header

To test with notification banner (if possible):
- If a persistence-warning banner is visible at the top, confirm the month navigator still anchors correctly below the header (not behind it)

Expected: Month navigator top edge touches the header bottom edge precisely while scrolled.
