# Milestone Sign-Off: v1.3 (Enhanced Debt Management)

## 🎯 Achievement Summary
Milestone v1.3 has successfully transformed the debt management experience from simple balance tracking to a full statement-to-payment lifecycle. Key achievements include the automation of payment tracking, high-precision PDF extraction across major UK banks, and integration of debt obligations into the cash flow forecast.

## ✅ Phase Completion
- **Phase 21 (Data Layer):** Successfully expanded schema to support linked statements and expenses.
- **Phase 22 (Debt UI):** Implemented advanced statement logging and automatic recurring expense generation.
- **Phase 23 (PDF Extraction):** Developed a robust, grid-aware PDF scanner supporting Lloyds, MBNA, TSB, Amex, and Nationwide.
- **Phase 24 (Payment Visuals):** Added specialized payment confirmation workflows and 💳 visual indicators.
- **Phase 25 (Forecast):** Fully integrated debt payments into the 90-day cash flow chart and daily planner.
