## Executive Summary

A complete rebuild of a monolithic budget app into a modular, offline-first PWA. All 91 active requirements were implemented and verified through automated tests and human UAT. The application now supports advanced UK-specific financial tracking including debt payoff planning, tax-free childcare, and account balance forecasting.

## Key Accomplishments

- **Modular Architecture:** Successfully refactored 800 lines of spaghetti code into a clean, ES6 module-based structure with a robust data layer (Dexie.js).
- **PWA & Offline Capability:** Implemented full offline support with service workers and a web manifest, making the app installable on desktop and mobile.
- **UK Bank PDF Import:** Developed a parsing engine for Barclays, HSBC, NatWest, Lloyds, and Santander statements with a manual mapping fallback.
- **Advanced Debt Planning:** Built Avalanche and Snowball payoff simulations with 0% promotional period tracking and monthly payment breakdowns.
- **Financial Forecasting:** Created a carry-forward balance engine that predicts future liquidity based on recurrent income and expenses.
- **Cloud Integration:** Added serverless backup and restore via Google Drive and Microsoft OneDrive.
