    - `addDeposit(accountId, date, amount, categoryId)`:

      7. **Add one-off expense**: Create a record in `oneOffExpenses` with note: `Tax-free Childcare: [ChildName]`, the deposit amount, and the provided `categoryId`.
    - `addSpend(accountId, date, amount, description)`:
      1. Add 'spend' entry to `childcareLedger`.
      2. Recalculate running balances.
  </action>
