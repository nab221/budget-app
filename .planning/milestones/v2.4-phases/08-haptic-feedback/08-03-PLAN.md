# Plan: Phase 8-03 - Secondary Haptic Feedback Integration

<objective>
Integrate haptic feedback into all remaining data-mutating actions in Debts, Assets, Targets, Subscriptions, and Childcare.
</objective>

<context>
@src/utils/haptics.js
@src/ui/debts.js
@src/ui/assets.js
@src/ui/targets.js
@src/ui/subscriptions.js
@src/ui/childcare.js
</context>

<tasks>
<task type="auto">
  <name>Integrate Haptics into Debts and Assets</name>
  <files>src/ui/debts.js, src/ui/assets.js</files>
  <action>
    - In `src/ui/debts.js`, import `triggerHaptic` and `alertWithHaptic`.
    - Replace `alert()` in `handleSaveDebt`, `handleSaveStatement`.
    - Add `triggerHaptic('success')` on successful save.
    - Add `triggerHaptic('delete')` in `deleteDebt` (after confirmation).
    - Repeat similar pattern in `src/ui/assets.js` for `handleSaveAsset` and `deleteAsset`.
  </action>
  <verify>Debt and Asset mutations trigger appropriate haptic patterns.</verify>
  <done>Debts and Assets provide tactile confirmation.</done>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Integrate Haptics into Targets and Subscriptions</name>
  <files>src/ui/targets.js, src/ui/subscriptions.js</files>
  <action>
    - In `src/ui/targets.js`, import `triggerHaptic` and `alertWithHaptic`.
    - Add `triggerHaptic('success')` in `saveBtn` callback on success.
    - Replace `alert()` in `saveBtn` error handler.
    - In `src/ui/subscriptions.js`, import `triggerHaptic` and `alertWithHaptic`.
    - Add `triggerHaptic('success')` in `handleAddSubscription`.
    - Add `triggerHaptic('delete')` in `deleteSubscription` (after confirmation).
    - Replace `alert()` in both.
  </action>
  <verify>Targets and Subscriptions mutations trigger appropriate haptic patterns.</verify>
  <done>Targets and Subscriptions provide tactile confirmation.</done>
</task>

<task type="auto">
  <name>Integrate Haptics into Childcare</name>
  <files>src/ui/childcare.js</files>
  <action>
    - Import `triggerHaptic` and `alertWithHaptic`.
    - Replace `alert()` in `_handleSaveAccount`, `_handleLogDeposit`, `_handleLogSpend`.
    - Add `triggerHaptic('success')` in these handlers on successful DB write.
    - Add `triggerHaptic('delete')` in `window.childcareDeleteAccount` (after confirmation).
  </action>
  <verify>Childcare mutations trigger appropriate haptic patterns.</verify>
  <done>Childcare tab provides tactile confirmation.</done>
</task>
</tasks>

<success_criteria>
- All secondary data-mutating actions provide tactile confirmation.
- Validation failures produce an 'error' vibration pulse.
- No direct navigator.vibrate calls exist outside of the utility.
</success_criteria>
