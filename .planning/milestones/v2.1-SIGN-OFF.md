- **Phase 2.1.1 (Security & Persistence):** Implemented three-tier UUID fallback (Crypto -> Math.random) and refined the persistence warning logic to suppress redundant banners when file-sync is active.

- **Phase 2.1.3 (Power-User Utilities):** Added manual "Trigger Recurrence" override and consolidated opening balance configuration into a single Settings block.
